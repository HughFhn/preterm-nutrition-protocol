# Background

## Glossary

- MoC = Model of Care
- DOL1 = Day Of Life 1
- Local Feed = Mother's Own Milk or Locally Sourced Breast Milk
- ml/kg/d = Millilitres of milk/ formula given per kg of the child per day
- MEBM = Mothers Expressed Breast Milk
- DEBM = Donor Expressed Breast Milk
- VLBR = Very Low Birth Rate
- NNU = Neonatal Unit
- GCP = Good Clinical Practice
- eHR = electronic Health Record
- EEG = Electroencephalogram
- EC = Ethics Comittee
- CA = Corrected Age
- CRF = Case Report Form
- Buccal Colostrum = Administering small amount of mothers milk (called colostrum) in the mouth of the preterm infant.  
- PN = Parenteral Nutrition
-Method of feeding that bypasses the gastrointestinal tract. Carbs, protein, fats are delivered into the bloodstream through an IV. Used when digestive system cant be used properly.
- EN = Enteral Nutrition
-Nutrition provided through the gastrointestinal tract using a tube, catheter or any intake possible. Delivers liquid formula containing essential nutrients into the stomach/ small intestine.
- TN = Transition Nutrition
-The gradual shift from one mode of nutrition to another. Typically from PN -> EN. To allow the gastrointestinal tract to adapt + resume normal function and ensuring nutritional needs are met.
- SPN = Standardised Parenteral Nutrition
Pre-formulated PN solution containing fixed amounts of macronutrients + micronutrients. Used when patients have common nutritional needs and don't require an individualised PN formula.

**Schedule 1 - Infants are at higher risk for feeding intolerance**

#### Feed Commencement

- Aim to feed within the first 24hr from birth if clinically appropriate.
- Give buccal colostrum if available. Dose of 0.1ml each side of the mouth every 3hrs for 48hrs / as directed by the medical team.

#### Feed Type

- **Preferred Type:** MEBM (*Fresh as available*)
- **Alternate:** DEBM

#### Initial Feeding Approach

- **Wait 24-48 hours after delivery** before considering DEBM
- Only use DEBM if Maternal Expressed Breast Milk (MEBM) is:
- Unavailable, or
- Insufficient

#### Feeding Progression

- **Continue DEBM until full feeds are established**
- Full feeds defined as 160 ml/kg/day
- **After reaching full feeds:**
- Switch to preterm formula (PTF)
- Formula selection depends on current weight:
- If current weight <1 kg: GP1
- If current weight >1 kg: NP1

#### Weaning Process

- **Aim to wean off DEBM gradually**
- **Weaning period: 3 days**

# National Preterm Standardised Parenteral Nutrition Model of Care

### Goals of Feeding the Preterm Infant:

- To achieve growth similar to foetal growth and obtain a functional outcome comparable to infants born at term.
- Optimising early protein intake may promote long-term brain health in preterm infants.

### Preterm PN Delivery in Ireland:

**Things that contribute to poor outcomes:**

- Limited IPN Service
- Highly dependant on IPN
- Nutritionally suboptimal SPN
- Gaps in Guidelines

### Preterm SPN Moc:

- Support transition from exclusive PN onto full milk feeds in preterm infants.
- Clinically proven and is a 2 part system of ;
-SPN Products
-Milk Feed Volume-based Protocol
- Designed for preterm infants  yet is safe for infants >32 weeks of GA and >1.5Kg and is the responsibility of the MDT (Multi Discipliniery Team) to ensure nutritional requirements are met.
- Universally designed to be used with any milk ffeed advancement protocol.
- Data-driven and based on preterm data from CUMH.

### What Does it do?

1. Delivers improved clinical & economical outcomes
2. Ensures safe achievement of nutrition recommendations as the infant moves from PN to full milk feeds.
3. Streamlines clinical care moving from a complex, individualised and high risk process to a standardised, safer, simpler system for patients and staff.

## Guide to Using SPN Protocol:

Step 1.
Asses if PN is indicated.

Step 2.
Asses suitability of SPN on a daily basis.

Step 3.
Determine the infant's milk feed volume as per local/ national Enteral Feed Advancement Guideline.

Step 4.
Refer to SPN Protocol to identify the nutrition phase (PN or TN) the infant is in based on the enteral feed volume.

### Deviation from Protocol:

==If fluid allowance is less than SPN protocol advises:==

1. Liberalise the daily fluid allowance as clinically acceptable.
2. Provide target lipid volume.
3. Reduce the aqueous volume within the total fluid allowance.
